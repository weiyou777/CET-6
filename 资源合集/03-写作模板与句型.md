# 六级写作模板与高分句型

> 提炼自 2016–2025 年六级作文真题话题与范文结构。

## 一、四大话题与对应立意库
| 话题 | 近年真题举例 | 常用立意 |
|---|---|---|
| 科技与社会 | AI与独立思考、社交媒体影响、科技对职场 | 工具属性、效率提升但防依赖、数字素养 |
| 青年成长 | 职业选择、平衡学业与兴趣、社会实践 | 个人实现、实践能力、终身学习的意义 |
| 社会发展与环境 | 环保生活、新能源 | 可持续发展、绿色生活方式 |
| 文化传承与责任 | 中国梦与青年责任、保护方言 | 个人与国家的联系、文化自信 |

## 二、三段式万能模板
**开头（2–3 句）**
> It is widely acknowledged that ______. From my perspective, ______.

**主体段 1（观点 + 解释 + 例子）**
> To begin with, ______. For example, ______. This demonstrates that ______.

**主体段 2（观点 + 解释 + 例子）**
> Furthermore, ______. Take ______ as an instance. Such experience helps ______.

**结尾（总结 + 升华）**
> In conclusion, ______. Only by ______ can we ______.

## 三、高分句型库
- `The significance of ... cannot be overstated.`
- `What matters most is that ...`
- `There is no denying that ...`
- `From my standpoint, ...`
- `Only by making continuous efforts can we ...`（倒装）
- `A case in point is that ...`
- `This phenomenon can be attributed to ...`（原因分析）

## 四、图表作文（2021 年考过）
- 描述趋势：`As is vividly shown in the chart, ... has witnessed a steady increase / decline.`
- 分析原因：`This trend can be explained by economic growth, policy support and changing mindsets.`
- 结尾预测：`It is predicted that this momentum will continue in the coming years.`

## 五、名言/谚语评论型（2017、2020 年考过）
- 引述：`The saying "______" conveys a profound truth.`
- 立论：`In essence, it emphasizes the importance of ______.`
- 例证 + 总结。

## 六、加分替换词
- important → vital / crucial / significant
- should → is supposed to / it is advisable to
- more and more → an increasing number of
- think → hold the view that / maintain
- use → utilize / make use of
