## 五、万能句式（直接套用）
1. 随着……的发展/提高：
   `With the development of...` / `With the improvement of living standards, ...`
2. 被誉为/被称为：
   `... is honored as / known as / praised as ...`
3. ……发挥了重要作用：
   `... has played a significant role in ...`
4. 不仅……也……：
   `not only ... but also ...`
5. 由于/因为：
   `due to / owing to / because of ...`
6. 占地/全长：
   `cover an area of ... / with a total length of ...`
7. 旨在/为了：
   `aim to / in order to / with the purpose of ...`
8. 数量描述：
   `hundred / thousand / million / billion`；`account for 20% of ...`

## 六、避坑提醒
- 时态：叙述历史事实用一般过去时；描述现状用一般现在时。
- 不会写的词用解释法（"旗袍"→ traditional Chinese dress）。
- 先写主干（主谓宾），再补修饰，保证语法正确优先于华丽。
